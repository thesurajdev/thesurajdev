How it works
1

Pin something

Select text and click "Pin selection". Or press Alt+Shift+K, hover to highlight an area, and click it. On ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, hover over a reply and use its pin button.

2

Add an icon and a note

Pick one of 16 icons (star, idea, question, decision, warning and more) and type a few words so you remember why you saved it. Both are optional.

3

Jump back

Open the Pins tab on the right edge, or press Alt+Shift+P, and click a pin. The page scrolls to the spot and flashes it. If the pin lives on another page, Pin & Jump opens that page first.

What else it does

Pinned spots stay visible

Everything you pinned on a page gets a thin blue outline, so you can see what you already saved.

Search and filter

Search notes and text, filter by icon, and switch between "This page" and "All pages".

Copy as Markdown

Export your pins as a list with links, ready to paste into notes, docs or a message.

Light, dark or match the site

The panel follows your browser's color scheme, copies the page you are on, or stays in the mode you choose.

What it isn't

Not a cloud service

There is no account and no sync. Your pins stay in your browser on this device.

Not watching your browsing

It asks for one permission, storage. It runs on pages so it can find your pins, and it sends nothing anywhere.

Not magic

Pins find their spot by matching text. If a page rewrites its content, the jump may land near the spot instead of on it, and Pin & Jump tells you when that happens.

Not full of trackers

No analytics, no ads, no remote code. Everything it runs ships inside the extension.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no server, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. We never see your pins, notes, or browsing activity, and it makes no network requests.

What is saved on your device

When you pin something, the extension saves it in your own browser using Chrome's local storage: the page address and title, a snippet of the text you pinned, your note and icon, the scroll position, and the time you pinned it. It also remembers your theme choice.

Because pins include page addresses and text from the pages you pin, treat them like your browser history: they stay on this device and are visible to anyone who uses your Chrome profile. Remove a pin with "Remove", or delete everything by uninstalling the extension.

Permissions we ask for

storage saves your pins and theme choice on your device.

Access to http and https pages lets the extension show its pin button and side panel, read the page text to find a pinned spot again, and outline pinned spots. It reads page content only on your device, and nothing it reads leaves the page.

It does not ask to read your history, your tabs list, or your bookmarks.

Where other services come in

Pin & Jump does not talk to any service. When you click a pin from another page, it opens that page's address in your browser, just as if you had typed it, and that site's own policy applies.

"Copy as Markdown" writes your pins to your clipboard only when you click it. Where you paste them is up to you.

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, and we do not use data for anything unrelated to the extension's single purpose, or for creditworthiness or lending.

Remote code

The extension does not load or run remote code. All of its code is included in the package you install.

Children

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
