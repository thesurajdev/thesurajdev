■ StatementFiled 2026

I don't just market brands. I build their growth.

Most marketers will run your campaigns and send you a report.

I do something different. I look at your business, diagnose what's actually blocking growth, and build the system that fixes it — a funnel, a tool, a brand, an automation sequence, or all of the above.

That's what I mean by Growth Builder. Not a fancier title. A different way of thinking.

Six years. 100+ brands across India. In every engagement the pattern was the same: the problem was never the channel. It was the missing system connecting them.

That's what I build.

— Suraj Dev
2019ENTRY 01

Entering the Workforce

Joined Matronics India Pvt. Ltd. as an Assistant. Unglamorous work — but it taught me discipline, documentation, and how businesses actually operate. The foundation everything else is built on.

2020ENTRY 02

The Accidental Beginning

During the COVID lockdown, Square Root Hospitality gave me a chance to build websites for their brands. I knew almost nothing — I learned by doing. HTML, WordPress, branding. Co-built Pepper Knife, a kebab brand, from food licensing to delivery partnerships.

Late 2020ENTRY 03

Co-founded AdZola

Knew I wanted to do this full-time. Co-founded AdZola — a performance marketing agency. Started with local gyms, salons, coaching centres. Grew into international clients. Paid ads, strategy, funnel building, client communication.

2021ENTRY 04

Going Beyond Ads

Stopped thinking in channels. Started thinking in systems. Explored SEO, automation, CRM, and content marketing. Took certifications, ran experiments, failed a lot, improved. Connected all parts of the digital engine.

2022ENTRY 05

Learning the Indian Business Mindset

Digital Marketing Specialist across education, wellness, and B2B. Learned how Indian founders think, what they actually care about, and how to connect marketing strategy to real sales outcomes.

2023ENTRY 06

Leading, Not Just Executing

Joined TechSharks as Digital Marketing Team Lead. First time managing a team and working across 100+ client projects. KPI setting, dashboards, accountability at scale.

2024 → NOWCURRENT

Building at Scale

Leading digital growth at Fortune Marketing Pvt. Ltd. — SEO, paid media, CRM, analytics, brand strategy. Working with PR, design, and dev teams to turn marketing into a revenue engine. Still building tools and brands on the side.
